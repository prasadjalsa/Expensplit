💡What is ExpenSplit?

ExpenSplit is a group expense tracker — for trips, flatmates, events, or any shared spending. It works like Splitwise but lives in a single HTML file you host on GitHub Pages.

  • No sign-up — access is controlled by a room link
  • Real-time sync — all members see changes instantly via Firebase
  • Flexible splits — equal, unequal, %, or share-based
  • Smart settlements — fewest possible transfers to clear all debts
  • Sign-off flow — each member acknowledges expenses before settling
  • Category icons — auto-suggested or manually picked per expense
  • Filter & sort — search, filter by person, sort by date or amount
  • CSV export — download full expense and settlement history
  • Dark mode — follows system preference, toggle in header
  • Installable — add to home screen on Android and iOS
🔗Rooms & Sharing

A room is a private shared workspace for your group. Every expense, balance, and settlement lives inside one room.

  • A random Room ID is created automatically when you first open the app
  • The Room ID appears in the URL as #room=abc123xyz and on this page
  • Anyone who opens the same URL joins the same live room
  • Data is stored in Firebase Firestore — it persists in the cloud, not just your browser
TipTap the 🔗 button in the header or Copy Link on the People tab to copy your room URL and send it to your group.
ImportantAnyone with the room link can view and edit the data — keep it within your group.
Creating a new room

Tap ＋ New Room on the People tab to open a brand-new empty room in a new browser tab. Your current room stays open and unaffected.

Always bookmark the full URLThe app identifies your room by the #room=abc123 part of the URL. If you bookmark or share just the base URL (without the hash), the app will open whichever room was last used on that browser — which may not be the right one. Always save or share the complete link.
👥How to: Set up a group
  1. Name the group
    Tap "Tap to add a name" in the header — type a name like "Goa Trip 2025" and press Enter. You can also do this via Settings ⚙️.
  2. Add all members
    On the People tab, tap + Add Member. In the modal, enter the person's name and at least one of: a UPI ID (e.g. name@upi) or a UPI QR code image. Tap Save Member. Repeat for every member.
  3. Share the room link
    Tap Copy Link on the People tab. Send the URL to your group — they open it and immediately join the same room.
TipAdd everyone before adding expenses — people can only be assigned to expenses if they exist in the group.
💳UPI ID & QR Code

Every member must provide at least a UPI ID or a QR code image so others can pay them during settlement.

Adding UPI details when adding a member
  1. Tap + Add Member on the People tab
  2. Enter the person's name
  3. Enter a UPI ID
    Type the VPA address, e.g. name@upi, 9876543210@okaxis. At least one of UPI ID or QR is required.
  4. Upload a QR code image (optional)
    Tap the upload area to pick a PNG or JPG from your device. Max size is 150 KB. A thumbnail preview appears.
  5. Tap Save Member
    The person appears in the list with their UPI badge and/or QR thumbnail.
Where UPI details appear
  • People tab — each member row shows a teal UPI badge (tap to copy the ID) and a QR thumbnail (tap to enlarge).
  • Settle Up tab — each suggested settlement shows a "Pay via UPI" strip with the payee's UPI ID and QR so the payer can scan directly.
TipTap the 📋 copy icon next to a UPI ID to copy it straight to your clipboard, then paste it into any UPI app.
NoteQR images are stored as base64 inside the Firebase document. Keep images under 300 KB to avoid slowing down sync.
🧾How to: Add an expense
  1. Go to Expenses tab and tap +
    The green + button is at the bottom-right of the screen.
  2. Enter description, amount, and date
    The app auto-picks an emoji icon based on keywords (e.g. "dinner" → 🍝). Date defaults to today.
  3. Set who paid
    Select the payer from the dropdown. The hint turns green when payer amounts match the total.
  4. Choose a split type
    Pick Equal, Unequal, %, or Shares and configure the amounts (see Split Types below).
  5. Tap Save Expense
    The expense appears instantly for all group members.
TipTap any expense card to view a full breakdown. From the detail view you can Edit or Delete it.
🎨Expense Category Icons

Every expense has a category icon shown on its card. When you type a description the app auto-suggests a matching icon — you can keep it or tap any icon in the grid to override it manually.

How it works
  1. Type a description
    The icon grid highlights the auto-suggested icon based on keywords (e.g. "dinner" → 🍝, "cab" → 🚗).
  2. Tap any icon to override
    Your choice is saved with the expense and shown on the expense card.
  3. Clear the choice
    Edit the expense and tap a different icon, or leave the description unchanged to revert to auto-suggest.

✅How to: Sign off

Before settling, each member confirms the expense list is complete and correct. This prevents disputes.

  1. Go to the Settle Up tab
    The Sign-offs card appears at the top with each member's status.
  2. Each member taps their own Confirm button
    Records the date of confirmation. Badge turns ✓ Confirmed.
  3. Once all confirmed, proceed to settle
    Summary shows "✓ All N members confirmed".

Sign-off statuses:

Pending — not confirmed yet.

✓ Confirmed — confirmed, no new expenses since.

⚠ N new expenses — confirmed earlier but new expenses were added after. Tap Re-confirm.

TipIf you tap Settle before everyone has signed off, the app will warn you with a list of members who are still pending or need re-confirmation. You can proceed anyway or go back and wait for them to confirm first.
TipOnce all members have confirmed, an Export CSV button appears on the Settle Up tab so you can download the full expense and settlement history.
💸How to: Record a payment

The Settle Up tab shows the minimum number of transfers needed to clear all balances.

  1. Review Suggested Settlements
    Each row shows who pays whom and how much.
  2. Make the payment outside the app
    Each settlement row shows a Pay via UPI strip with the payee's UPI ID (tap 📋 to copy) and QR code (tap to enlarge and scan). Transfer via GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, or any UPI app.
  3. Tap Settle on that row
    Pre-filled dialog opens. Adjust amount or add a note (e.g. "GPay"), then tap Confirm Payment.
  4. Repeat until all transfers are recorded
    App shows 🎉 All settled up! when every balance is cleared.
TipThe smart settlement algorithm minimises transfers. 5 people with complex debts may need only 4 (or fewer) payments to fully balance out.
Edit a recorded paymentIn the Settlement History section, tap the ✏️ pencil icon next to any payment to edit its amount, date, or note. Tap ✕ to delete it.
📥How to: Export CSV

Once every member has confirmed their sign-off, an Export CSV button appears on the Settle Up tab. Tap it to download a spreadsheet with three sections:

SectionWhat's included
ExpensesDate, description, amount, split type, who paid, and how it was split per person
BalancesNet balance for each member (positive = gets back, negative = owes)
Settlement HistoryAll recorded payments — from, to, amount, date, and note
TipThe file is named GroupName_expenses.csv and opens in Excel, Google Sheets, or Numbers.
NoteThe Export button only appears when all members have confirmed sign-offs. If it's not visible, check the Sign-offs card at the top of the Settle Up tab for anyone still pending.

⚙️How to: Settings
Change group name

Tap "Tap to add a name" in the header to edit inline, or open Settings ⚙️ → Group / Expense Name.

Change currency symbol

Open Settings ⚙️ → Currency Symbol. Type your symbol (e.g. $ € £ ¥) and tap Save. All amounts update immediately.

Remove a member

On the People tab, tap ✕ next to the person's name. If they appear in any expense you'll be asked to confirm.

Edit a member's UPI details

On the People tab, tap the ✏️ pencil icon next to a member's name. The same Add Member modal opens pre-filled — update the UPI ID or upload a new QR image, then tap Save Member.

Clear All Data vs Delete Room

Clear All Data — wipes expenses, settlements, and sign-offs but keeps the room alive with all members, group name, and currency intact. The room link still works. Use this to reuse the same group for a new trip.

Delete Room — permanently removes the entire room from Firebase including members, expenses, and the room itself. The room link stops working for everyone. Use this to fully close a room you no longer need.

Cannot be undoneBoth actions are irreversible. Take a screenshot of Settlement History before proceeding if you need a record.

📲Install as App

Expensplit can be installed to your home screen and launched like a native app — no App Store required.

On Android (Chrome)
  1. A 📲 Install button appears in the header
    Tap it when prompted by the browser.
  2. Tap Install in the browser prompt
    The app is added to your home screen and opens in standalone mode (no browser chrome).
On iPhone (Safari)
  1. Open the app in Safari
  2. Tap the Share button (box with arrow)
  3. Tap "Add to Home Screen"
    The app icon appears on your home screen and opens fullscreen.
NoteThe 📲 Install button only appears on browsers that support the install prompt (Chrome on Android, Edge on desktop). On iOS you must use the Safari share sheet.
🔄How to: Reuse for a new trip
  1. Record all payments first
    Go to Settle Up and tap Settle on every suggested transfer to preserve the history.
  2. Open Settings ⚙️ and tap Clear All Data
    Expenses and settlements are deleted. Group name, members, and currency are kept.
  3. Update the group name
    Tap the name in the header and type the new trip name.
  4. Start adding new expenses
    Same room ID — no need to re-share the link.
❓FAQ
Is my data private?

Data is secured by obscurity — only people with the room link can access it. The Room ID is a random 12-character string that is practically impossible to guess. Don't share the link publicly.

What if I lose the room link?

The Room ID is saved in your browser's local storage. Opening the app on the same browser/device reconnects automatically. The Room ID is also shown on the People tab — copy it and store it safely. On a new device, ask a group member with the link to share it again.

Can multiple people add expenses at the same time?

Yes. Firebase Firestore syncs in real time. When any member saves an expense, all other open browsers update within seconds. The ● Live pill confirms the connection is active.

What does ● Live / ● Offline mean?

● Live — connected to Firebase, changes sync in real time.

● Offline — Firebase connection failed. The app shows the last saved state from your browser. Changes won't sync until reconnected.

How does the smart settlement work?

The app calculates each person's net balance (total paid minus total owed). It then uses a debt-minimisation algorithm to find the fewest transfers that bring every balance to zero — significantly fewer than settling every pair individually.

Can I edit or delete an expense after saving?

Yes. Tap the expense card to open its detail view, then tap Edit or Delete. Adding or deleting expenses marks previously confirmed sign-offs as needing re-confirmation.

What does "Re-confirm needed" mean?

A member confirmed the expense list earlier, but new expenses were added (or deleted) after that. The ⚠ N new expenses badge prompts them to review and tap Re-confirm if they agree.

Does the app work without internet?

Partially. Existing data loads from browser local storage when offline. Changes won't sync until reconnected. Firebase may also buffer writes locally and replay them when the connection is restored.

What happens when I tap Clear All Data?

Expenses, settlements, and sign-offs are permanently deleted from both the browser and Firebase. The group name, member list (including UPI details), and currency symbol are preserved — the room stays alive and the link still works. This is different from Delete Room, which removes everything including members and the room itself. Cannot be undone — take a screenshot of Settlement History first if you need a record.

Can I use this for multiple trips at once?

Yes — each room is a separate workspace. Tap ＋ New Room on the People tab to open a fresh room in a new browser tab. Share that room's link with the relevant group. Always bookmark or share the full URL including the #room= part so everyone lands in the right room.

How many people can be in a group?

There is no hard limit. Groups of 2–20 people work well. For larger groups, note that every person's QR image is stored in a single Firestore document (1 MB limit) — keep QR images under 150 KB each to stay well within that limit.

Is a UPI ID or QR code mandatory?

Yes — at least one of a UPI ID or a QR code image is required when adding a member. This ensures the payer always has a way to transfer money during settlement. You can add both if you like. Use the ✏️ edit button on the People tab to update these details at any time.

The % input keeps unchecking the person — what do I do?

Tap directly on the number input box, not on the row background. The row background acts as a checkbox toggle. Targeting the input itself avoids accidentally deselecting the person.

Can I change the currency mid-trip?

Yes — open Settings ⚙️ and update the currency symbol. This is a display-only change; underlying numbers are not converted. If your group used two currencies, convert amounts manually before entering.
