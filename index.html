💡What is Expensplit?

Expensplit helps groups track shared spending — trips, flatmates, events, dinners. Add expenses, see who owes what, and settle up with the fewest possible payments. No account needed.

  • No sign-up — just share the room link with your group
  • Everyone sees the same data — changes show up instantly for all members
  • Flexible splits — equal, unequal, by % or by shares
  • Smart settlements — fewest possible transfers to clear all debts
  • Sign-off flow — everyone confirms the list before settling
  • Category icons — auto-picked or chosen manually per expense
  • Filter & sort — search, filter by person, sort by date or amount
  • CSV export — download the full expense and payment history
  • Dark mode — follows system preference, easy on the eyes
  • Activity log — full history of who added, edited, or deleted expenses
  • Notifications — get notified when expenses involving you change
  • Works like an app — add to your home screen on Android or iPhone
🔗Rooms & Sharing

A room is your group's private space. All expenses, balances, and payments live inside it. Everyone who opens the same link sees the same data — live.

  • A room is created automatically when you first open the app
  • Your room link is shown on the People tab — tap Copy Link to share it
  • Anyone who opens the link joins the same room instantly
  • Data is saved in the cloud — it's not lost if you close your browser
TipTap the 🔗 button in the header or Copy Link on the People tab to copy your room link.
Keep the link privateAnyone with the link can view and edit the room. Only share it with your group.
Starting a new room

Tap ＋ New Room on the People tab. A fresh empty room opens in a new tab — your current room is untouched.

Always share the full linkThe room is identified by the end of the URL (the part after #room=). If you share just the website address without that part, whoever opens it will get a brand new empty room instead of yours. Always copy and share the full link.
👥Setting up your group
  1. Name the group
    Tap "Tap to add a name" in the header and type something like "Goa Trip" or "Flat expenses". Press Enter to save.
  2. Add everyone in the group
    Go to the People tab and tap + Add Member. Enter their name and UPI ID or QR code so others can pay them later. Repeat for each person.
  3. Share the room link
    Tap Copy Link and send it to your group via WhatsApp, iMessage, or wherever you chat. They open the link and are in the same room immediately.
TipAdd everyone before logging expenses — you can only split costs with people who are already in the group.
💳UPI ID & QR Code

Each member needs a UPI ID or QR code so others can pay them when settling up. At least one is required.

  1. Tap + Add Member
  2. Enter their name
  3. Enter their UPI ID
    This is the address they use to receive money — e.g. name@upi or 9876543210@okaxis.
  4. Optionally upload their QR code
    Tap the upload area and pick a photo of their UPI QR code. Max 300 KB. A small preview appears.
  5. Tap Save Member
TipTap the 📋 icon next to a UPI ID to copy it. Tap a QR thumbnail to enlarge it and scan directly from the Settle Up screen.
Edit anytimeOn the People tab, tap the ✏️ pencil next to a member's name to update their UPI ID or QR code.
🧾Adding an expense
  1. Go to the Expenses tab and tap +
    The + button is in the bottom-right corner.
  2. Enter a description, amount, and date
    The app suggests a category icon based on what you type. Date defaults to today.
  3. Set who paid
    Choose the person (or people) who paid. The green hint confirms the amounts add up correctly.
  4. Choose how to split it
    Pick Equal, Unequal, % or Shares — see Split Types below for details.
  5. Tap Save Expense
    Everyone in the room sees it immediately.
TipTap any expense to see the full breakdown. You can edit or delete it from there.
🎨Category Icons

Every expense shows an icon on its card. The app picks one automatically based on what you type — tap any icon in the grid to choose a different one.

  1. Type a description
    The app highlights a suggested icon — e.g. "dinner" → 🍝, "cab" → 🚗.
  2. Tap any other icon to pick it instead
    Your choice is saved with the expense.
✂️Split Types

💳When multiple people paid

Use this when two or more people split a bill at the counter — e.g. Alice paid ₹300 and Bob paid ₹200 for a ₹500 meal.

  1. Enter the first payer and their amount
  2. Tap "+ Add payer" and enter the second person
  3. Check the hint turns green
    The amounts must add up to the total before you can save.

✅Sign-offs

Before settling up, everyone confirms they've seen and agreed with the expense list. This avoids disputes later.

  1. Go to the Settle Up tab
    The Sign-offs card shows each person's status at the top.
  2. Each person taps their own Confirm button
    Their badge turns green — ✓ Confirmed.
  3. Once everyone has confirmed, settle up

What the badges mean:

Pending — hasn't confirmed yet.

✓ Confirmed — agreed, and nothing has changed since.

⚠ Expenses changed — confirmed earlier but the expense list changed since. Tap Re-confirm.

TipIf you tap Settle before everyone has confirmed, the app shows a warning listing who still needs to sign off. You can still proceed if needed.
TipOnce everyone confirms, Export CSV and Print / PDF buttons appear so you can save a copy of everything.
💸Settling up

The Settle Up tab shows the smallest number of payments needed to clear every balance.

  1. Check the Suggested Settlements
    Each row shows who pays whom and how much.
  2. Make the payment outside the app
    Each row shows a Pay via UPI strip — tap 📋 to copy the UPI ID or tap the QR thumbnail to scan it in GPay, PhonePe, or any UPI app.
  3. Tap Settle on that row
    A form opens pre-filled with the amount. Add a note if you like (e.g. "GPay") and tap Confirm Payment.
  4. Repeat for each payment
    When all balances are cleared, the app shows 🎉 All settled up!
TipThe app works out the minimum number of payments — 5 people with tangled debts might need just 4 transfers.
Paying in instalments?You can enter less than the full amount when recording a payment. The settle form shows how much has already been paid and how much is still remaining for that pair.
Made a mistake?In the Settlement History section, tap ✏️ next to any recorded payment to edit it, or ✕ to delete it.
📥Exporting & Printing

Once everyone has signed off, two buttons appear on the Settle Up tab:

ButtonWhat it does
🖨️ Print / PDFOpens a clean printable summary in a new tab. Use your browser's Save as PDF to save it. Works on all devices including iPhone (Share → Print → Save to Files).
📥 Export CSVDownloads a spreadsheet with three sections — Expenses, Balances, and Payment History. Opens in Excel, Google Sheets, or Numbers.
Not seeing the buttons?They only appear when everyone has confirmed their sign-off. Check the Sign-offs card at the top of the Settle Up tab.
📋Activity Log

The Activity tab shows a full history of every expense that was added, edited, or deleted — who did it and when.

Setting up your identity

When you open the app for the first time, you'll be asked "Who are you?" — pick your name from the group. This lets the app highlight activity that involves you and send you relevant notifications.

TipYou can change your identity at any time from the Activity tab — tap the Change button next to your name.
NoteYour identity is saved on this device only. If you open the app on a different phone or browser, you'll be asked again.
🔔Notifications

Turn on notifications to get an alert whenever an expense involving you is added, edited, or deleted.

  1. Set your identity first
    Go to the Activity tab and select who you are. The app needs to know which person you are to send you relevant alerts.
  2. Open Settings ⚙️ → Notifications → Turn On
    Your browser will ask for permission — tap Allow. A quick test notification confirms it's working.
Android & Desktop — Notifications fire even when the app is in the background.
iPhone — Notifications work when the app is installed to your home screen (via Safari Add to Home Screen) and the app is open or recently used.
Blocked?If you tapped "Block" by mistake, go to your phone's Settings → Safari or Chrome → find this site and allow notifications.

⚙️Settings
Change group name

Tap the name in the header to edit it directly, or open Settings ⚙️ and change it there.

Change currency symbol

Open Settings ⚙️, update the Currency Symbol (e.g. $ € £ ¥), and tap Save. All amounts update straight away.

Remove a member

On the People tab, tap ✕ next to their name. If they're part of any expense, you'll be asked to confirm first.

Edit a member's payment details

On the People tab, tap ✏️ next to their name to update their UPI ID or QR code.

Clear All Data vs Delete Room

Clear All Data — removes all expenses, payments, and sign-offs but keeps the group members, name, and currency. The room link still works. Good for starting a new trip with the same group.

Delete Room — permanently removes everything, including members and the room itself. The link stops working for everyone. Use this when the room is no longer needed.

Both actions cannot be undone. Export a CSV or take a screenshot first if you want a record.
🌙Dark Mode

Tap 🌙 in the header to switch to dark mode. Tap ☀️ to go back. Your choice is remembered every time you open the app.

TipThe app automatically matches your phone's dark/light setting on the first visit. You can override it any time using the toggle.
📲Installing the app

Add Expensplit to your home screen so it's always one tap away — no App Store needed.

On Android
  1. Tap the 📲 Install button in the header
    This appears automatically in Chrome when the app is ready to install.
  2. Tap Install when asked
    The app icon appears on your home screen. It opens fullscreen and supports notifications.
On iPhone
  1. Copy your room link first
    Tap 🔗 in the header or Copy Link on the People tab to copy the full room link.
  2. Tap the Share button in Safari (box with arrow pointing up)
  3. Tap "Add to Home Screen"
  4. Open the app from your home screen
    On first launch you'll see a welcome screen — paste your room link there and tap Join Room. You won't be asked again.
iPhone tipCopy your room link before installing so you have it ready for the first launch. Use Safari — Chrome and other browsers on iPhone don't support Add to Home Screen.
🔄Reusing for a new trip
  1. Record all payments first
    Go to Settle Up and tap Settle on every suggested transfer so nothing is lost.
  2. Export or print a summary if you want a record
    Once everyone has signed off, tap Export CSV or Print / PDF on the Settle Up tab.
  3. Open Settings ⚙️ and tap Clear All Data
    All expenses and payments are removed. Members, group name, and currency stay.
  4. Rename the group
    Tap the name in the header and type the new trip name.
  5. Start adding new expenses
    Same room link — no need to share it again.
❓Common Questions
Is my data private?

Only people with the room link can see or edit the data. The link contains a random code that's practically impossible to guess. Don't share it publicly.

What if I lose the room link?

Your browser remembers the room and reconnects automatically when you reopen the app on the same device. You can also copy the Room ID shown on the People tab and keep it somewhere safe. On a new device, ask someone in the group to share the link again.

Can multiple people add expenses at the same time?

Yes. Changes show up for everyone within seconds. The green ● Live badge in the header means you're connected and everything is in sync.

What does ● Live / ● Offline mean?

● Live — connected, changes sync in real time.

● Offline — no connection. The app shows the last saved data. Changes won't reach others until you're back online.

How does the smart settlement work?

The app adds up everything each person paid and everything they owe, then works out the smallest set of transfers to bring everyone to zero. This is usually far fewer payments than settling each pair of people individually.

Can I edit or delete an expense after saving?

Yes. Tap the expense card to open it, then tap Edit or Delete. Changing the expense list will prompt anyone who already signed off to re-confirm.

What does "Expenses changed" mean on a sign-off?

That person confirmed the list earlier, but something was added or removed since then. They need to review and tap Re-confirm to agree to the updated list.

Does the app work without internet?

Partially. You can view existing data, but any changes won't reach others until you're back online. The app will sync automatically once the connection is restored.

What happens when I tap Clear All Data?

All expenses, payments, and sign-offs are deleted. Your group members, name, and currency are kept — the room link still works. This is different from Delete Room, which removes everything including members. Export a CSV first if you want a record.

Can I use this for multiple trips at once?

Yes — each room is a separate space. Tap ＋ New Room on the People tab to open a fresh room for a different group or trip. Always share the full link (including the code at the end) so everyone joins the right room.

How many people can be in a group?

There's no hard limit. Groups of 2–20 work well. If you have many members each with a large QR image, keep the images under 300 KB each to avoid slowing things down.

Is a UPI ID or QR code required?

Yes — at least one is required per member so others can pay them during settlement. You can add both. Tap ✏️ on the People tab to update these at any time.

The % input keeps deselecting the person — what do I do?

Tap directly on the number input box, not on the row around it. Tapping the row toggles that person in or out of the split.

Can I change the currency mid-trip?

Yes — open Settings ⚙️ and change the currency symbol. This only changes how amounts are displayed, not the numbers themselves. If your group used two currencies, you'll need to convert amounts manually before entering them.
